What is pixel bending?

Every digital image is simply a long list of numbers. In the Effects and 3x3 Grid tabs, Bent Pixel reshapes live Red, Green, and Blue numbers directly in memory so you can see how color channels, row buffers, and brightness sorting behave.

In the Hex Editor tab, you open the raw compressed bytes of a real JPEG file. Because JPEG compresses each 8x8 tile relative to the tile before it, changing a single byte in the middle of the picture creates color shifts and tears that cascade down the frame.

Anatomy of a JPEG file

Click any block above to jump straight to those bytes in the Hex Editor beside this window.

Header
Marks the start of the file (FF D8) and declares image width, height, and channels. Breaking it stops the browser from decoding.
Tables
Quantization and Huffman tables that tell the decoder how to translate compressed bits back into color values.
Picture Data
The safe playground. Header Lock keeps edits inside this scan stream so wild glitches happen without crashing the file.
End
The closing FF D9 signature marking the exact end of the compressed image stream.

Pioneers of glitch art and databending

1965
Nam June Paik - TV Magnet

Placed an industrial magnet atop a CRT television to pull its electron beam into glowing arcs, pioneering deliberate electronic distortion.

1978
Jamie Fenton & Raul Zaritsky - Digital TV Dinner

Popped a Bally Astrocade cartridge mid-load to record crashing video RAM onto tape, creating one of the earliest digital glitch films.

1990s
JODI - Joan Heemskerk & Dirk Paesmans

Turned raw HTML, broken browser windows, and machine errors into landmark net.art exposing the hidden substrate of the early web.

2000
Kim Cascone - Post-Digital Aesthetics

Named the post-digital movement, celebrating buffer drops, clicks, and file errors as expressive musical and visual material.

2010
Rosa Menkman - Glitch Studies Manifesto

Showed how compression formats like JPEG and GIF reveal their true hidden geometry at the exact moment they fracture.

2012
Kim Asendorf - Pixel Sorting

Popularized sorting rows of pixels by brightness intervals, turning rigid digital grids into cascading painterly streaks.
