Nollie

macOS + iOSPublic beta ↗
(opens in a new tab)

Nollie is a crew of helpers for the busy bits of life: tickets, trips, bills and plans. They work on your Mac at home and answer to your iPhone and Apple Watch. Sending, buying, posting and deleting always wait for your yes.

Use a model on your Mac, or a ChatGPT, Claude or other plan you already have. Nollie has no servers, no accounts and no analytics.

Built natively in SwiftUI on Hermes Agent by Nous Research.

In public beta on Mac, iPhone and Apple Watch.

NOWHERE: Be Somewhere

iOSApp Store ↗
(opens in a new tab)

A private, personal place journal for iPhone. Check in to places you visit, watch your Fog Map clear as you explore, and keep a quiet record of your life in places.

Built natively in SwiftUI on Core Location, MapKit, SwiftData and iCloud, with Cap running on Apple Intelligence and no servers of its own.

No accounts, no feeds, no tracking, no ads.
